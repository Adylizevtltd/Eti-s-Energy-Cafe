Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to follow an extreme diet?+
The program focuses on personalized nutrition guidance and practical lifestyle habits. Your coach can discuss an approach appropriate to your individual needs.
Can beginners join the program?+
Yes. You can discuss your current activity level, experience, and goals with the coach to understand which activities may be suitable for you.
Do I need to join a gym?+
The available activities and exercise guidance can be discussed with the coach based on your preferences and individual circumstances.
How do I choose between the Basic and Complete plans?+
Contact Eti on WhatsApp to discuss your goals, lifestyle, and the support available under each program.
How can I get started?+
Simply send a WhatsApp message to 7278366751 and share your weight-management or wellness goals.
Will I get a personalized diet plan?+
The program offers customized nutrition guidance. The coach can explain the available guidance and how it fits your individual needs.
How much weight can I lose?+
Weight-loss results vary from person to person. Your progress depends on individual circumstances, health, nutrition, activity, and consistency. The coach can discuss realistic goals without promising a specific result.
Can I discuss my health concerns before joining?+
Yes. Share your concerns with the coach before beginning. If you have a medical condition, take medication, or need medical nutrition therapy, consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
