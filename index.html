Yes. Share your current activity level, experience, and goals to understand which activities may suit you.
Do I need to join a gym?+
Available activities can be discussed based on your preferences and circumstances — no gym required.
How do I choose between Basic and Complete?+
Message Eti on WhatsApp to discuss your goals, lifestyle, and the support available under each program.
How much weight can I lose?+
Results vary from person to person, depending on health, nutrition, activity, and consistency. Eti can discuss realistic goals without promising a specific result.
Can I discuss health concerns before joining?+
Yes. If you have a medical condition or take medication, also consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
